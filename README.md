## Who I am

**YC Eagle (轶辰)** — founder & director of [LILAC Puzzle](https://linktr.ee/lilacpuzzle) (est. 2022) and a practitioner-researcher in digital media & game studies.

I work at the intersection of `game design theory` · `interactive media` · `puzzle mechanics` · `rapid prototyping`. Core focus: **Hanzi-based puzzle systems**, **hybrid-reality / ARG mechanics**, and the idea that *a puzzle can work as a medium* — for learning, connection, and cultural expression.

## By the numbers

> `5000+` community members · `10k+` daily messages · `~1M` Bilibili plays · `250k+` reach on one research write-up · `50k+` views in 3 days (theory recap) · `100+` teams (first on-site school hunt) · `5` cities for Puzzlers' Day · `300+` original articles

## Selected facts & honors

- **Research & translation** — adapted Prof. Hiroyuki Iida's *Game Refinement Theory* with a multi-disciplinary team (initiated direct contact with his lab; obtained original papers); produced public-facing write-ups and an observer report for an e-games academic conference.
- **Talks & publications** — invited speaker at *Celebration of Mind* (G4G16, China) on online puzzle-community practice (case: LILAC); journal article *Guess, Cut, Rearrange: Hanzi Factory* (2025).
- **Hackathon & product** — AdventureX 2026, team "Mo Yu Shuang" product-operations lead; built **Tunta**, a zero-trust snapshot-based retrieval extension — featured by Cyzone.
- **Teaching** — core instructor & academic host, Perk Summit 2026; designed a PBL workshop on campus-based multimedia interactive puzzle hunts.
- **Community & events** — founded NAPCA (school puzzle clubs) and the first National School Puzzle Hunt (NSPH); lead organizer of Puzzlers' Day (5 cities); initiator of *China Puzzle Collection*; co-initiator of China Puzzle Award.
- **Media & publishing** — co-planned a Bilibili rhythm-game variety series (~1M plays); editor of the sci-fi magazine *Future Dream*; co-creator of a math-and-art magazine; 9 years of content operations with 1M+ cumulative views.

## Exploring now

- A city-scale hybrid-reality ARG concept: mapping Hanzi radicals onto real city coordinates/objects; players "gather" character roots by moving through physical space and combine them via on-device recognition, GPS and NFC — turning the city itself into a live glyph board.
- Turning AI-agent workflows into real game-dev and community-automation pipelines; picking up more programming (TypeScript, WebGL/HTML5).
- Quantitative game-design research: applying Game Refinement Theory to player-engagement modelling.
